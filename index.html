My name is Dr. Minina Johnson (Dr. Jaye). Some of you may know me from my recent service as your Western Region Chief of Staff during the last biennium under Regional Syntaktes Andrea R. Magee Davis. For those I have not yet had the pleasure of meeting, here is a bit about my Sigma journey and my life outside of it.

I came into Sigma Gamma Rho Sorority, Inc. in Spring 2016 through Delta Pi Sigma Chapter in Macon, GA, as a proud Tr3. I am a Life Member, #2417, and I currently hold membership with Iota Beta Sigma Chapter in Silicon Valley, CA. I live in Vail, AZ, and by profession I hold a doctorate in business administration with a specialization in program management, along with over 20 years of experience leading large-scale programs and building operating systems that help organizations run smoothly. I bring that same lens of structure, care, and follow-through to this role.

Spring 2016 · Delta Pi Sigma, Macon GA Tr3 Life Member #2417 Iota Beta Sigma · Silicon Valley, CA

My goal over the next two years is simple: to make sure every undergraduate chapter in the Western Region feels supported, informed, and set up to thrive. That starts with me getting to know you, not the other way around.

Because I am new to many of you, I want to begin by listening rather than dictating. Over the coming weeks, you will receive a short survey to help me understand your chapter's strengths and needs, an invitation to virtual office hours where you can meet me directly, and this newsletter, on repeat.

Please do not hesitate to reach out with any immediate needs, questions, or concerns. I am here to serve you, and I take that responsibility seriously.
