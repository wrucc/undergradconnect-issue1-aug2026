👋🏽 HEY WR UNDERGRADUATES AND ADVISORS! 👋🏽

Welcome to the 2026-2027 Sorority Year! 🎉


A new sorority year means new goals, new connections, and a fresh chance to show up for one another. I am excited for what this year holds for every chapter in the Western Region, and I am glad to be walking into it alongside you.

I am honored to introduce myself as your new Undergraduate Chapter Coordinator, and I wanted to reach out personally before we get into the business of the next two years together.

I am Dr. Minina Johnson, but please call me Dr. Jaye. Some of you may know me from my recent service as your Western Region Chief of Staff during the last biennium under Regional Syntaktes Andrea R. Magee Davis. For those I have not yet had the pleasure of meeting, here is a bit about my Sigma journey and my life outside of it.

Spring 2016 · Delta Pi Sigma, Macon GA
💙💛 Tr3 Club 💛💙
Legacy
Life Member #2417
Current Member of Iota Beta Sigma · Silicon Valley, CA

I joined Sigma Gamma Rho Sorority, Inc. in Spring 2016 through Delta Pi Sigma Chapter in Macon, GA, as a proud Tr3 🐩. I am Life Member #2417, and I currently hold membership with Iota Beta Sigma Chapter in Silicon Valley, CA. I live in Vail, AZ, and by profession I am the Founder and Principal Strategist of Greater Ambitions LLC, a project management and operations consultancy. I hold a Doctorate in Business Administration with a specialization in Project Management, along with over 20 years of experience leading large-scale programs and building operating systems that help organizations run smoothly. I bring that same lens of structure, care, and follow-through to this role.

What draws me to this work is simple. Undergraduate chapters are where Sigma Gamma Rho comes alive every single day: on campuses, in social media, and in the moments of service and sisterhood that do not always make it into a program summary. I do not take for granted that I am stepping into spaces many of you have built and sustained long before I arrived.
My job is to support that work, not redirect it.

My goal over the next two years is to make sure every undergraduate chapter in the Western Region feels supported, informed, and set up to thrive. That starts with me getting to know you.

Because I am new to many of you, I want to begin by listening to you!
Below is a short survey to help me understand your chapter's strengths and needs, invitations to virtual office hours where you can meet me directly, and this newsletter, on repeat.

Please do not hesitate to reach out with any immediate needs, questions, or concerns.
I am here to serve you, and I take that responsibility seriously.
Here is to a strong start and an even stronger finish, together.

2026–2027 Undergraduate Programming Guide

The purpose of this programming guide is to help our undergraduate chapters take the guesswork out of being impactful without being overextended.
